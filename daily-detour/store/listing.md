## Description
Plan a delivery round through a miniature town. Draw your courier’s route along the streets: collect orders from the bakery, florist, grocer and ice-cream parlour, deliver them to the right doors, and ride home.

Every route is a small puzzle. Ice cream melts if you dawdle. Roadworks close the obvious way. One-way streets decide which way round you go. On some rounds your basket only holds two, and riding past a shop with a full basket can be exactly the clever detour you need.

- A new daily route for everyone, gentle on Mondays and trickiest at the weekend
- 140 routes in the route book, from first lessons to tricky rounds
- Gold, silver and bronze stamps, with the gold target always shown
- Hints that work from your own route: a strategic nudge, the next objective, or one step
- Restore your village with the hearts your deliveries earn
- Seasonal events: Blossom Week, the Summer Fete, the Harvest Fair and Winter Lights
- No timers, no lives, no energy. Missing a day is a rest day and never breaks your run.
